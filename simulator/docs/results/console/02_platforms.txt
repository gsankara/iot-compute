Read it as three different limits, not three scores.  The float is
slow in z but sustainable; the drone is fast everywhere and running
down a battery from the moment it lifts off; the tethered head is fast,
free to the node and tiny -- and pays for it in the fibre it trails,
which blocks other nodes' beams.
