The swarm converges far more slowly than its thrusters could manage,
and the charge column says why: a 10 cm^2 cell at 10 m depth harvests
around 40 mW while a thruster draws 500, so the float cannot power
continuous motion.  It moves in bursts and spends the rest of the time
recharging.  That is a constraint the motion planner has to respect --
travel time here is set by the energy budget, not by terminal velocity.
